I.O.N

Privacy Policy

App: I.O.N (Chaturanga)  ·  Developer: Rudraneel Samanta
Last updated: 13 November 2026

In short: ION has no account. The developer does not collect, track or sell your personal data. Your game progress stays on your device, or in your own private Google Drive app storage if you choose to link it. The developer cannot read it.

1. About this policy

This policy explains what happens to your data when you play I.O.N (also written ION), a game made by Rudraneel Samanta (“the developer”, “I”). The game is free to play, works offline, and needs no account.

2. Data that stays on your device

ION saves the following on your own device, so the game can remember where you left off:

  • your game progress and settings, including your language choice;
  • your Sol Gems (the in-game currency) and unlocked cosmetic items;
  • your list of purchase history shown in the Trade screen;
  • the fact that you accepted the Terms of License.

This data is not sent to the developer or to any server of the developer. There is no ION server, no account and no sign-in. Clearing the app’s data or uninstalling ION removes this data from your device.

3. Optional Google Drive backup

If you choose to link Google Drive, ION saves and loads a backup of your game so you can restore it later or on another device. This is optional. The game works fully without it.

  • ION asks for only one Google permission: drive.appdata.
  • This permission lets ION use only its own hidden application data folder (appDataFolder) in your Google Drive. ION uses it only to save and load your game backup file.
  • ION cannot view, edit or delete any other files in your Google Drive, and you will not see this folder in your normal Drive files.
  • The developer cannot read your backup. It is stored in your own Google account, and the data is used only to restore your game for you.
  • ION does not sell this data, does not share it with anyone, and does not use it for advertising.

ION’s use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

4. Ads and purchases (coming soon)

Rewarded ads, Sol Gems purchases and the shop are not available in ION yet and are coming soon. Nothing in this section is active today.

Once they launch, the following will apply:

  • When you choose to watch a rewarded ad, the ad provider (Google AdMob) may collect device and ad information, such as your advertising ID and IP address, and may use it to choose ads, as described in its own privacy policy. The developer does not control this data.
  • When you buy something, the store (such as Google Play) handles the payment under its own policies. The developer never sees your card or payment details.
  • You can reset your advertising ID or turn off ad personalisation in your device settings.

5. Children

ION does not collect personal data from anyone, including children. Once purchases are available, anyone under the age of majority where they live should ask a parent or guardian before buying anything.

6. Deleting your data

  • On your device: clear ION’s data in your device settings, or uninstall the app.
  • In Google Drive: unlink Google Drive in ION’s Settings. You can also remove the backup yourself in your Google account: Google Drive → Settings → Manage apps → ION → Delete hidden app data.
  • Need help? Email the developer (see below) and ask about deleting your data.

7. Contact

If you have questions about your privacy or data, or want to ask about deleting your data, contact the developer at ion_strategy@googlegroups.com.

If you write to this address, the developer sees your email address and your message and uses them only to reply to you. They are not sold or shared.

8. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated, for example when new features such as ads or purchases are added. The “Last updated” date at the top shows when it last changed. If the Terms of License change in a way that matters, ION asks you to read and accept them again.
